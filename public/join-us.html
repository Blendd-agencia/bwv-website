Open position

Accounting Assistant

Lisbon, Portugal
Permanent contract
Full-time
€14,000 – €18,000

Main Responsibilities

  • Support the Certified Accountant in daily accounting management
  • Organise, classify and record accounting documents
  • Perform bank reconciliations and support supplier account management
  • Monitor fixed assets, accruals and deferrals
  • Prepare billing processes and provide tax support, including withholding taxes, SAF-T, CIT, VAT and other related obligations
  • Communicate with clients and suppliers by phone and email

Profile We Are Looking For

  • Degree in Accounting, Management or a similar field
  • Knowledge of Primavera software
  • Good Excel skills
  • Fluency in Portuguese (C1/C2); English is valued
  • Minimum of 2 years’ experience in similar roles
  • Organised, responsible and team-oriented profile

What We Offer

  • Attractive salary package, including meal allowance, transport allowance and health insurance
  • Permanent contract after the initial period, when applicable
  • Allianz Health Insurance, including outpatient care, hospitalisation and dental plan
  • Flexible working hours, with the possibility of occasional remote work
  • International, collaborative and multidisciplinary work environment
  • Continuous training to support your technical and professional growth
  • Modern and well-located office in central Lisbon, with excellent public transport access
