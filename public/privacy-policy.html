Privacy Policy

How BWV handles personal information submitted through the website.

Draft page structure. The final legal wording has not been supplied in the client source material and must be reviewed and approved by BWV’s legal/privacy advisers before launch.

1. Data controller

The final policy must identify the legal entity acting as data controller and its full company details.

2. Personal data collected

This section should cover data supplied through contact and proposal forms, correspondence and any technical information collected through the website.

3. Purposes of processing

The final policy should explain why personal data is processed, including responding to enquiries, preparing proposals and managing professional relationships.

4. Legal bases

Applicable legal bases under GDPR must be confirmed by BWV’s privacy advisers for each processing purpose.

5. Data retention

Final retention periods or the criteria used to determine them must be supplied and approved before launch.

6. Recipients and processors

Any service providers, hosting partners, analytics providers or other recipients of personal data must be accurately listed where legally required.

7. International transfers

If personal data is transferred outside the EEA, the final policy should identify the relevant safeguards.

8. Your rights

The approved policy should explain applicable rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and the right to lodge a complaint.

9. Contact and updates

Privacy contact details and the effective date/version history will be added once the final policy is approved.
