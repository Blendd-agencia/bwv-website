Our approach

A systematic, cost-effective process.

  1. Review and understand client’s document process flow, information system, and reporting needs.
  2. Customise report formats according to client’s needs.
  3. Plan a schedule to meet submission deadline/s.
  4. Adopt a systematic and cost-effective process that starts from data entry to completion of accounts.
Our services include

  • Monthly financial statement inclusive of balance sheet

  • Profit and loss statement
