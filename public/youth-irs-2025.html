Youth IRS 2025:
what changes and who can benefit

The new regime extends the tax benefit to more young workers in Portugal.

From 2025 onwards, the Portuguese Youth IRS regime introduces new rules with a direct impact on employment and self-employment income. The regime has been expanded in terms of age, duration and exemption limit, making it relevant for young professionals, employers and payroll teams.

What changed in the Youth IRS regime in 2025?

The maximum age increases to 35

The benefit may now apply to young taxpayers up to the age of 35, assessed on 31 December of the year to which the income relates.

The maximum duration increases to 10 years

The regime may apply for a maximum period of 10 years of income from employment or self-employment, provided that the taxpayer continues to meet the legal requirements.

Academic qualification is no longer a requirement

Access to the Youth IRS regime no longer depends on the completion of a specific education level, making the benefit broader and easier to understand.

The exemption limit increases to 55 times the IAS

In 2025, the maximum exemption threshold corresponds to 55 times the Portuguese Social Support Index (IAS), equivalent to €28,737.50.

Applicable exemption percentages

The exemption is applied progressively over the benefit period:

  • 100% in the 1st year of income
  • 75% from the 2nd to the 4th year
  • 50% from the 5th to the 7th year
  • 25% from the 8th to the 10th year

Who can benefit?

The Youth IRS regime may apply to taxpayers up to the age of 35 who receive employment or self-employment income and who are not considered dependants in a household for tax purposes.

Who is excluded?

The regime does not apply in certain cases, including taxpayers who benefit or have benefited from the Non-Habitual Resident regime, the tax incentive for scientific research and innovation, the tax regime for former residents, or taxpayers whose tax situation is not regularised.

How can the Youth IRS regime be requested?

The benefit can be claimed in the annual IRS tax return. A young worker may also ask the employer to consider the benefit monthly through payroll withholding tax, indicating the year in which they first earned income as a non-dependant taxpayer.

Why is this change relevant for companies?

Companies should pay close attention to this regime, particularly when employees request its application through payroll withholding. Clear communication between the employee, employer and accounting team helps prevent payroll processing errors and supports correct tax compliance.

BWV recommendation

BWV recommends that young workers confirm their eligibility before requesting the regime. For companies, it is important to validate the information received from employees and ensure that payroll processing correctly reflects the applicable exemption percentage.

Conclusion

The 2025 Youth IRS regime represents a significant change in the taxation of young workers in Portugal. The extension of the age limit, duration and exemption ceiling may result in meaningful tax savings, provided that the regime is correctly applied.
